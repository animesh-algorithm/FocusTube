A NOTE FROM ME

Thank you for
using FocusTube.

I built FocusTube because I kept running into the same problem: I'd open YouTube to watch something useful, then get distracted by everything around it. I just wanted a way to stay with the video I'd chosen.

It was a personal project to solve that problem. Then other people started using it, and some of you took the time to leave such kind reviews.

Reading those words means a lot to me. Thank you for trying FocusTube, for sharing what helped, and for letting me know when something didn't work.

I really appreciate it.
