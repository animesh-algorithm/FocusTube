# Focus Tube privacy policy

Focus Tube hides selected elements on YouTube pages. It does not collect browsing history, page content, personal information, or usage analytics, and it does not send any data to the developer or a developer-controlled server.

The extension stores your on/off choices for Shorts, comments, related videos, descriptions, and channel information with `chrome.storage.sync`. Chrome may sync those choices through your Google account according to your browser settings. The developer cannot access those stored choices.

The extension reads YouTube page links and DOM elements in your browser only to identify distractions to hide. It does not transmit them elsewhere.

You can remove stored preferences by uninstalling the extension or clearing its extension storage. Questions can be sent through [GitHub issues](https://github.com/animesh-algorithm/FocusTube/issues).

Last updated: September 26, 2026.
